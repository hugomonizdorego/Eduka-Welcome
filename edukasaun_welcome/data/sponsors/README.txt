Sponsor and partner logos for the Welcome Screen (page 6).

Put PNG, SVG, JPG or WebP files here (or in /usr/share/edukasaun-welcome/sponsors,
or ~/.config/edukasaun-welcome/sponsors for testing) and list them in project.json:

  "sponsors": [{"name": "Example Foundation", "logo": "example.png", "url": "https://example.org"}]

Only approved logos may be added. Plain names ("Example Foundation") also work.
